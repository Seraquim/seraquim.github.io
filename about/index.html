About

The medium changes

SERAQUIM is the creative identity of a multidisciplinary designer working across graphic design, identity, digital experiences, image-making, motion and visual experiments.

SERAQUIM is the name I work under. Behind it is one designer who never quite settled on a single discipline — trained in graphic design, pulled sideways into type, then motion, then screens, then a run of packaging jobs that taught me more about paper than any class did.

I don't think of that as doing everything. I think of it as following the idea. A brand might need a typeface more than a logo; a campaign might need to move more than it needs to be printed. The work is noticing which — and then doing that part properly.

Most projects are with small teams and independent organisations: presses, shops, festivals, founders. Places where the designer still sits close to the decisions.

How I work

Look longer than feels necessary. Try the wrong thing on purpose. Build systems that other people can use without me in the room. Care about the half-millimetre details nobody will consciously notice — they're the ones that make something feel considered.

The medium changes. The visual thinking doesn't.
