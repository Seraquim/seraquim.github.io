Project 001 — Identity, Editorial

Low Tide Editions

An identity for a small poetry press that publishes four books a year and refuses to rush any of them.

Year
2025
Discipline
Identity, Editorial
Client
Low Tide Editions
Role
Identity, book design
Tools
InDesign, Glyphs, Risograph

Brief

A press with four titles a year and no marketing budget needed something that would look the same on a spine, a tote bag and an Instagram square — and still feel unhurried.

Approach

The tide became the system. Each book gets a pattern drawn from the tide chart of the day it went to print, so the series builds into a record of its own making. Type stays quiet: one serif, one size for titles, generous margins.

Outcome

Twelve covers in, the system hasn't needed a single exception. The press now briefs its printers with a date instead of a design.
