Project 002 — Campaign, Motion

Signal & Noise

Posters, titles and a moving identity for an electronic music festival, built from interference patterns.

Year
2025
Discipline
Campaign, Motion
Client
Signal & Noise Festival
Role
Art direction, motion
Tools
After Effects, TouchDesigner, Illustrator

Brief

A three-day festival of electronic music wanted an identity that could move, sound and print — without becoming another gradient on a poster.

Approach

Two fields of fine lines laid over each other produce interference that never resolves the same way twice. The same rule drives posters, stage visuals and twelve-second social loops.

Outcome

One rule, forty formats. The motion system ran live from the stage desk, reacting to the music in the room.
