Work

Visual output

Six projects across identity, print, motion, screens and objects. Each one is laid out the way it asked to be.

  • Low Tide Editions

    Identity, Editorial — 2025

    An identity for a small poetry press that publishes four books a year and refuses to rush any of them.

  • Signal & Noise

    Campaign, Motion — 2025

    Posters, titles and a moving identity for an electronic music festival, built from interference patterns.

  • Ferro

    Typography, Identity — 2024

    A seventy-year-old hardware store, rebuilt around a display face cut from the shapes of its own tools.

  • Halflight

    UI, UX — 2024

    A sleep and light app designed to be looked at for as short a time as possible.

  • Common Ground

    Packaging — 2023

    Packaging for a coffee roaster: a cut-paper composition for every origin, and a box that folds without glue.

  • Orbit Studies

    3D, Personal — 2025

    A running series of wireframe planets. One rule: every object is drawn with fewer than thirty lines.
