Project 004 — UI, UX

Halflight

A sleep and light app designed to be looked at for as short a time as possible.

Year
2024
Discipline
UI, UX
Client
Halflight
Role
Product design
Tools
Figma, Rive, Protopie

Brief

A sleep app that doesn't want your attention. Halflight dims your lights on a schedule; the interface should be finished with you in under ten seconds.

Approach

One control per screen. The light curve is drawn as dots rather than a gradient so it stays legible at the lowest brightness. Every animation is slower than feels natural — on purpose.

Outcome

Average session length went down. That was the goal.
