Project 005 — Packaging

Common Ground

Packaging for a coffee roaster: a cut-paper composition for every origin, and a box that folds without glue.

Year
2023
Discipline
Packaging
Client
Common Ground Coffee
Role
Packaging, illustration
Tools
Illustrator, cutting plotter, Blender

Brief

A roaster buying from six farms wanted each origin to feel distinct on the shelf, without six different brands.

Approach

Each origin gets a cut-paper composition made from the same five shapes, in its own palette. The box is a single sheet that locks shut with a tab — no glue, no plastic window.

Outcome

One printing plate for the structure, six for the compositions. The roaster now adds a new origin by cutting paper, not by commissioning a redesign.
