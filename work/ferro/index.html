Project 003 — Typography, Identity

Ferro

A seventy-year-old hardware store, rebuilt around a display face cut from the shapes of its own tools.

Year
2024
Discipline
Typography, Identity
Client
Ferro Hardware
Role
Type design, identity
Tools

Brief

A family hardware store, seventy years on one street, losing ground to warehouses. The owners wanted to look like themselves again — not like a startup.

Approach

Every letter of the display face is assembled from the outlines of tools on the shop wall. Width became the brand: narrow for shelf labels, wide for the fascia, the same letters in between.

Outcome

New fascia, labels, vans and a typeface the family owns outright. Regulars say the shop finally looks the way it always felt.
