03

How a project moves

  1. 01

    Observe

    Understand the problem, the people, the culture around it and the visual language it already speaks.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Go wide. References, sketches, compositions, small ugly tests. Quantity first; taste later.

  3. 03

    Distort

    Question the obvious answer. Swap the medium, break the grid, combine things that shouldn't fit.

  4. 04

    Build

    Turn the strongest idea into a system — type, colour, components, motion — that other people can use.

  5. 05

    Refine

    Spacing, kerning, timing, materials, edge cases. The part nobody notices when it's done right.

  6. 06

    Release

    Put it into the world, watch how it behaves, adjust. A system is finished when it holds up in use.

About

One designer, moving between mediums. The medium changes; the visual thinking doesn't.

Some weeks that means a typeface. Other weeks it's a packaging net, a campaign in forty formats, or a planet drawn with eighteen lines. The method stays the same: look closely, try the wrong thing on purpose, then build something that holds up when other people use it.
